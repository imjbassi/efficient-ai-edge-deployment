\title{Efficient Packaging and Deployment of AI Models for Edge Inference}
\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Jaiveer Bassi}\IEEEauthorblockA{College of Science, Engineering, and Technology\\Grand Canyon University, Phoenix, Arizona, USA\\jaiveerbassi@yahoo.com}}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
Edge deployment requires more than a compact neural-network artifact: the model, runtime, service boundary, and resource policy must be reproducible as one system. This paper presents an open implementation that combines MobileNetV2, post-training integer quantization, optional magnitude pruning, a FastAPI inference service, and Docker packaging. We describe the pipeline, its operational interfaces, and a reproducibility-oriented benchmark protocol. The repository contains a reference benchmark profile reporting a reduction in model storage from 13.6\,MB to 3.3\,MB and mean inference latency from 220\,ms to 95\,ms; these values are preserved as reference/emulated results rather than presented as newly verified measurements. Energy values are derived from latency and an assumed power profile. We therefore separate measured, derived, and emulated quantities and identify the calibration, hardware, and dataset artifacts required for a publishable empirical evaluation.
\end{abstract}
