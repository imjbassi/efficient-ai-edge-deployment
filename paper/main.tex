\begin{abstract}
Deploying state-of-the-art Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) on resource-constrained edge hardware remains a formidable challenge due to severe limitations in memory, computing power, and energy. This paper presents an end-to-end engineering methodology that co-optimizes neural network compression with streamlined containerized edge packaging. Using MobileNetV2 pre-trained on ImageNet as a primary case study, we evaluate the joint impact of 30\% structured magnitude weight pruning and full INT8 Post-Training Quantization (PTQ). Our optimized model delivers a 4.1x reduction in model storage size (from 13.6 MB to 3.3 MB), a 3.7x reduction in active memory footprint (from 240 MB to 65 MB), and a 2.32x acceleration in mean CPU inference latency (from 220 ms to 95 ms) on a Raspberry Pi 4 Model B, while experiencing a negligible drop in Top-1 accuracy (from 71.8\% to 71.3\%). Furthermore, we analyze simulated energy per inference, showing a 2.47x improvement in energy efficiency (from 1.42 to 3.51 inferences per Joule). Finally, we demonstrate how this pipeline is successfully operationalized through a microservice architecture built on FastAPI and docker-compose, establishing a robust, production-ready framework for edge MLOps.
\end{abstract}
