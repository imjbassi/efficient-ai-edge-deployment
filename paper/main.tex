\large A Reproducible MobileNetV2 FP32-to-INT8 Study}

\begin{abstract}
Edge inference systems require a compact model and a reproducible path from conversion to serving. This paper presents an open MobileNetV2 deployment artifact that generates comparable FP32 and full-integer TensorFlow Lite models, evaluates both on the complete 3,925-image Imagenette validation split, measures single-thread host-CPU latency, and serves the INT8 artifact through FastAPI and Docker. Calibration uses 200 deterministically selected training images. On an AMD Ryzen 5 7600 with TensorFlow 2.15.1 and one TFLite thread, integer quantization reduced the model from 13.34 to 3.81 MiB (3.50$\times$), reduced mean inference latency from 8.53 to 6.46 ms (1.32$\times$), and changed top-1 accuracy from 66.73\% to 65.81\% (a 0.92 percentage-point decrease). The artifact records raw latency samples, hashes, preprocessing, versions, and dataset provenance. These host results establish the conversion pipeline; device-specific latency, energy, and container overhead remain separate measurements for target edge hardware.
\end{abstract}
