\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

INT8 was reliably smaller, but it was not intrinsically faster or accurate. In this MobileNetV2 audit, per-channel quantization delivered useful accuracy and a 1.43$\times$ one-thread XNNPACK speedup, while per-tensor quantization destroyed accuracy and built-in kernels made INT8 slower than FP32. The result replaces a blanket ``quantization accelerates inference'' narrative with a testable deployment rule: validate the model's granularity and benchmark the exact backend, thread policy, and serving boundary that will run in production.

The repository turns that rule into a repeatable audit. It retains per-image paired predictions, process-level latency replicates, raw request timings, hashes, host controls, a hardened container, and the scripts that generated the tables and figure. Stronger external validity now requires replication across additional architectures, full ImageNet, native ARM systems, and energy-controlled edge hardware rather than additional claims from the present host.
